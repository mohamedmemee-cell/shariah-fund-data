A new fund is not used by the Portfolio Builder immediately. It first goes through research, source automation, validation and only then becomes Active.

Submitting creates a candidate request in GitHub. The fund should remain inactive until its performance data, TER, minimum investments, TFSA eligibility and current Shariah governance have been verified and its data source can be maintained reliably.
